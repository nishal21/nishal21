Handwriting texture
  handwriting.png: letter from Abraham Lincoln to Mr. Brayman, September 23, 1854.
  U.S. National Archives (NARA 192847), public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Abraham_Lincoln_Letter_to_Mr._Brayman_September_23,_1854_-_NARA_-_192847.jpg
  Cropped and turned into a transparent ink layer.

Visitor song voice and music
  The visitor song is sung by ACE-Step 1.5 (acestep-v15-turbo) by ACE Studio x StepFun.
  https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5  License: Apache-2.0.
  Each take comes from acemusic.ai, the ACE-Step team's free hosted API (https://acemusic.ai), or, when that is
  unavailable, from the same open model running on a GitHub Actions CPU. The player, the lyrics page and the video
  credits name which one made the current take. Voice and music are separated with Demucs (MIT, Meta) for the
  vocal-forward mix, and the lyrics are timed with faster-whisper (MIT) using OpenAI Whisper (MIT).
