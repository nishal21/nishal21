Visitor song voice and music
  The visitor song is sung by ACE-Step 1.5 (acestep-v15-turbo) by ACE Studio x StepFun.
  https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5  License: Apache-2.0.
  Each take comes from acemusic.ai, the ACE-Step team's free hosted API (https://acemusic.ai), or, when that is
  unavailable, from the same open model running on a GitHub Actions CPU. If there is no take yet, Kokoro sings
  instead. The player, the lyrics page and the video credits name which one made the current take. Voice and
  music are separated with Demucs (MIT, Meta) for the vocal-forward mix, and the lyrics are timed with
  faster-whisper (MIT) using OpenAI Whisper (MIT).

Contribution song voice
  The contribution song uses pinned Runware takes (assets/song-voices.json and assets/song-pins.json).
  The rap (Original) is MiniMax Music 2.6 for the male, female and duet voices.
  The acoustic is ACE-Step v1.5 XL for the male and female voices, and MiniMax Music 2.6 for the duet.
  Voices rotate each day in Asia/Kolkata, starting 19 Nov 2026: male rap with female acoustic, then female
  rap with male acoustic, then both songs as a male and female duet. While a style is pinned, the nightly
  job leaves its audio and timing alone and only updates the page and the calendar numbers. If a take is
  not pinned, Kokoro sings (two voices). The lyrics page, the card and contribution-song.json name the
  current engine.
